What sort of lodging do you offer?

We have Bungalows and Townhomes. All units open directly to the outdoors.

Our Bungalows are freestanding one-room studio-style units. Singles have 1 king bed. All others have 1 king and 1 queen bed. They vary in size from 430 sq ft to 540 sq ft, much larger than a standard hotel room! All bungalows have a private covered porch with a table & chairs, kitchenette, and drive up convenience.

Our Townhouses are privately owned with fully equipped kitchens, separate bedrooms (1, 2, and 3 bedrooms), some with sleeping lofts, covered balconies & patios, BBQ grills, outdoor seating, washer & dryer, as well as attached covered parking. Unit specific sleeping arrangements are listed on the booking page.

Please call us and we're happy to assist you in choosing what's best for you. We also include a list of in-unit amenities, max occupancy, pictures and other helpful details for each unit on our booking page.

What amenities are on-site and what's included with my stay?

You will have full access to all our amenities at no extra charge. Amenities include our Meadow (private park), pool, hot tub, basketball court, walking trail and Horsethief Coffee. NOTE: Horsethief Coffee is seasonal. Open March 1 - the Sunday after Thanksgiving.

The Meadow is our most unique amenity and most treasured by all our guests. It's our private park with fresh flowing cool spring water, wading pools, ponds, large native trees that provide plenty of filtered shade, cool grass, hammocks, fire circle and views of the sandstone cliffs.

We have Moab’s cleanest and most scenic heated swimming pool (16 by 42 feet - open mid- March through mid-October) and a large (10 foot by 10 foot) in-ground jetted hot tub which is open year round.

We have a loop trail on site and viewpoints of the Portal of the Colorado River. We are a bike-in bike-out resort with direct connection to the Moab pedestrian path that leads you to the River Road, Arches National Park, and beyond.

In addition to full use of our resort, you will receive personal recommendations, a daily porch service (bungalows only), washer and dryer (townhomes only), free parking up to 2 vehicles, WiFi, cable, large Smart TV's, air conditioning and heat.

Horsethief Coffee is our on-site cafe. Hours vary by season. We have our own baker and offer a wide array of coffee drinks, cold teas and lemonades, and a great grab and go breakfast menu including fresh made breakfast sandwiches, burritos, fruit, fresh granola, oatmeal and an every changing variety of freshly made baked goods. Some of them are gluten free and vegan.

We encourage you not to miss what everyone raves about -rediscovering the old-fashioned pleasures of wading in fresh cool spring water, relaxing in the shade on a lounge chair or hammock, and bobbing in a cool refreshing pond. All of this wonder and relaxation can be found in our private meadow.

What are our safety standards and procedures?

Moab Springs Ranch remains committed to exceeding the minimum standards required of all hotels and resorts. Nothing can eliminate all risk of transmission of various illnesses, but when we can use common sense to avoid unnecessary risks, we do so. To keep our community, visitors and staff safe, we offer the following:

  • The pool and hot tub are open. Limit is 30 guests on a first come basis.
  • Distancing between family parties is suggested. Masking not required on our property, however masks are available at our front desk if you feel symptomatic.
  • Virtual registration, optional no-contact check-in, self check-out, no-contact service and maintenance.
  • All cleaning products used in unit cleans are disinfectants.
  • Hand sanitizer dispensers are mounted on the interior of every accommodation, office, and building on our property.
  • No-contact daily porch service for amenity replenishment.
  • Staff: Staff with symptoms are not permitted to work and cannot return to work without testing for Covid.
  • We ask that guests respect other guests by maintaining a social distance of 10 feet from other guest parties, to be on the safe side.
  • We ask that guests inform us if they have symptoms or have been notified they've been exposed prior to arriving at Moab Springs Ranch and while staying with us, so we can take measures to protect our staff and quarantine the unit they stayed in.

How does Arches National Park Timed Entry work?

Arches National Park Timed Entry system is designed to promote a safer and more enjoyable visit, increase access to parking and trailheads, protect cultural and natural resources, and improve the visitor experience by reducing congestion.

Timed Entry is only in effect from 7am-4pm between April 1-July 6 and August 28-October 31.

Your Timed Entry ticket is good for a two hour slot. You must enter the park within your two hour allotted window. Please take into consideration that there may be a line to enter the park. You may be permitted to enter the park outside of your two hour window if you were in line prior to the end of your time slot. There is no limit for your time spent in the park on the day of your entry.

Reservations can be made:

  • Online at rec.gov
  • By phone 877-444-6777 (domestic)
  • By phone 606-515-6777 (international)
  • By phone 877-833-6777 (TDD)

Reservations can be made online up to 6 months in advance or after 7pm MDT the night before your visit (a second batch of tickets are released at this time). Unbooked tickets will be available for same day visits, but they will not be available at the entrance station. You must acquire your Timed Entry ticket prior to arriving at the entrance station.

Please note that visitors are responsible for park entrance fees in addition to their Timed Entry ticket. Your ticket is free, but there is a $2 processing fee.

If you have a camping reservation or backpacking permit, you do not need a Timed Entry reservation.

Learn more by visiting the Timed Entry FAQ on the official Arches National Park website.
