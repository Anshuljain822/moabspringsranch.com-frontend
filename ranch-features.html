RANCH FEATURES & GUEST EXPERIENCES

From relaxing by the pool to exploring walking trails beneath cottonwood trees, Moab Springs Ranch offers thoughtfully designed spaces that help guests connect with nature, recharge, and enjoy everything the property has to offer.

OUTDOOR POOL & HOT TUB

Relax after a day of adventure in our heated pool and hot tub, surrounded by the peaceful landscapes and open skies of Moab.

Natural Springs & Meadow

Discover the springs and green spaces that help create the unique oasis-like atmosphere found throughout the ranch.

Dark Sky Viewing

Experience Moab's remarkable night skies and enjoy peaceful evenings beneath a canopy of stars far from city lights.

Horse Thief Coffee Shop

Start your day with locally roasted coffee, fresh offerings, and a welcoming gathering space located right on the ranch.

Historic Ranch House

One of the property's most recognizable landmarks, reflecting the history and character that continue to shape the ranch experience today.

Bike Trail Access

Ride directly from the ranch to many of Moab's most popular cycling routes and outdoor adventures.

Walking Trails

Explore peaceful pathways that wind throughout the property, offering opportunities to slow down and enjoy the natural surroundings.

Sustainable Design

Thoughtful planning and environmental stewardship help preserve the beauty and character of the ranch for future generations.
