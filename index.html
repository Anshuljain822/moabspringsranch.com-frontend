Discover a Desert Oasis Where Moab’s Greatest Adventures Begin and End

Moab Springs Ranch offers a rare blend of natural beauty, comfort, and connection in the heart of Utah’s red rock country. Surrounded by springs, cottonwood trees, and open desert landscapes, our property is designed for travelers who want more than just a place to stay. Here, you’ll find spacious bungalow and townhouse accommodations, peaceful outdoor spaces, and easy access to Moab’s most iconic destinations including Arches and Canyonlands National Parks.

Whether you’re here for adventure, relaxation, or a bit of both, Moab Springs Ranch is your basecamp for experiencing everything Moab has to offer.
