EXPLORE MOAB: PARKS, TRAILS & OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Moab is one of the most remarkable outdoor destinations in the American Southwest. From towering sandstone arches and dramatic canyon vistas to scenic drives, hiking trails, mountain biking routes, and river adventures, there is no shortage of ways to explore. Whether you're visiting for a weekend getaway or an extended stay, Moab Springs Ranch places you close to the experiences that make this destination unforgettable.

ARCHES NATIONAL PARK

Home to more than 2,000 natural stone arches, Arches National Park is one of Utah's most iconic landscapes. Visitors can explore scenic viewpoints, hiking trails, and world-famous formations including Delicate Arch and Landscape Arch.

CANYONLANDS NATIONAL PARK

Vast canyons, mesas, and dramatic overlooks define Canyonlands National Park. Divided into distinct districts, the park offers endless opportunities for hiking, sightseeing, photography, and exploration.

DEAD HORSE POINT STATE PARK

Known for its breathtaking overlooks and panoramic canyon views, Dead Horse Point State Park offers some of the most photographed scenery in Utah.

FISHER TOWERS

These towering red rock formations provide one of the area's most unique hiking experiences and are a favorite destination for photographers and climbers alike.

LA SAL MOUNTAINS & HIDDEN LAKE

Escape the desert heat and discover alpine scenery, forested trails, and cooler temperatures in the nearby La Sal Mountains. Hidden Lake offers a peaceful mountain experience unlike anywhere else near Moab.

COLORADO RIVER ADVENTURES

Experience Moab from a different perspective with rafting, kayaking, paddleboarding, and scenic river excursions along the Colorado River.

MOUNTAIN BIKING

Moab is internationally recognized as one of the world's premier mountain biking destinations, with routes available for beginners and experienced riders alike.

HIKING & NATURE WALKS

From short interpretive trails to challenging backcountry routes, hikers of every skill level can find memorable experiences throughout the region.

STARGAZING

Moab's dark skies create exceptional opportunities for astronomy, photography, and simply enjoying the beauty of the night sky.
